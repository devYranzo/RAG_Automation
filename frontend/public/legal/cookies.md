# Cookie Policy - TalentFinder

**Last Updated:** May 25, 2026

---

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to enable essential platform functionality.

---

## 2. Types of Cookies Used

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for the platform to function:

- Authentication tokens (JWT)
- Session management
- Tenant isolation context
- Security validation

---

## 3. Legal Basis

We use strictly necessary cookies based on:

- Legitimate interest
- Technical necessity for service operation

No consent banner is required for strictly necessary cookies.

---

## 4. Cookies We Do Not Use

We do NOT use:

- Advertising cookies
- Third-party tracking cookies
- Behavioral analytics cookies
- Marketing pixels

---

## 5. Data Stored in Cookies

Cookies may store:

- Session identifiers
- Authentication tokens
- Tenant context information

No sensitive personal data is stored directly in cookies.

---

## 6. Retention

Cookies are stored only for the duration necessary:

- Session cookies expire when browser is closed or session ends
- Authentication cookies expire after a defined security period

---

## 7. Security

Cookies are protected using:

- HttpOnly flag
- Secure flag (HTTPS only)
- SameSite policies

---

## 8. Managing Cookies

Users may disable cookies via browser settings. However, disabling them may prevent the Service from functioning correctly.
