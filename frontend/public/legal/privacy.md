# Privacy Policy - TalentFinder

**Last Updated:** May 25, 2026

---

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller is the entity operating TalentFinder.

Contact: privacy@talentfinder.ai

---

## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all personal data processed through the TalentFinder platform, including CVs, user queries, and system logs.

---

## 3. Legal Basis (GDPR Article 6)

We process personal data under the following legal bases:

- Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
- Legal obligation (where applicable)

---

## 4. Data We Process

We may process:

### Candidate Data

- CV documents (PDF)
- Professional experience
- Skills and education

### User Data

- Account identifiers
- Search queries
- Usage activity logs

### Technical Data

- IP address (for security)
- Session identifiers
- System logs

---

## 5. Purpose of Processing

We process data to:

- Provide AI-based candidate ranking
- Enable semantic search over CVs
- Improve system performance and reliability
- Ensure security and abuse prevention

---

## 6. AI Processing and Profiling

TalentFinder uses automated systems to:

- Analyze CV content
- Generate embeddings
- Rank candidates based on relevance

These processes are assistive only and do not produce legally binding or final employment decisions.

---

## 7. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for:

- Service operation
- Legal compliance
- Security monitoring

Users may request deletion at any time.

---

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data may be shared only with:

- Cloud infrastructure providers
- AI model providers (e.g. LLM APIs)
- Database and hosting providers

All providers are bound by data protection agreements.

---

## 9. International Transfers

If data is transferred outside the EEA, we ensure appropriate safeguards such as:

- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
- Adequacy decisions where applicable

---

## 10. Data Subject Rights (GDPR)

Users have the right to:

- Access their data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)
- Restrict processing
- Object to processing
- Data portability

Requests can be sent to: privacy@talentfinder.ai

---

## 11. Security Measures

We implement:

- TLS encryption in transit
- Multi-tenant data isolation
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging
- Secure token authentication

---

## 12. Data Breach Notification

In case of a personal data breach:

- We will notify affected users without undue delay
- We will notify supervisory authorities where required by law
- We will take corrective security measures immediately

---

## 13. Automated Decision-Making

TalentFinder does not make fully automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement.

Users remain responsible for final hiring decisions.
